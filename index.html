    05 / THE DEFOPS FRAMEWORK

    Operational claims.
    Testable evidence.

    DEFOPS asks whether your team can contain failures and explain what happened, within a defined deployment boundary.

    A review gate,
    not an average.

    Assess seven controls against a shared rubric. Patching, revocation and reconstruction are mandatory. A strong result elsewhere cannot compensate for one of these failing.

    Every applicable control needs a passing test, evidence, an owner, a date and a documented result. Noncritical exclusions need an owner and rationale.

    The evidence rubric

    UNKNOWN IS NOT ZERO
    ?

    Unassessed

    No rating recorded. The outcome remains unknown.

    0

    Absent or failed

    The control is missing or the test failed.

    1

    Defined

    A process exists but has not passed a representative test.

    2

    Exercised

    Passed a representative exercise with supporting evidence.

    3

    Deployed & reviewed

    Passed in deployed conditions with a named reviewer.

    Seven controls to examine

    SCOPE / TEST / EVIDENCE

    What the result means

    Incomplete

    Scope, assessments or required evidence are still missing.

    Needs remediation

    A control failed, a rating is below the threshold, or evidence needs a retest.

    Ready for review

    The recorded requirements are complete. A human still needs to review the evidence and decide.

    DEFOPS is a decision support framework, not a validated predictor of security outcomes or a certification. Evidence freshness is an adjustable policy. The tool checks the record you enter; it does not inspect evidence sources or validate operational claims.
