    About DEFOPS
    
      
    
      
    
        
    What DEFOPS is
    
        
    
          DEFOPS (Defense Operations) coverage asks a simple operational question:
          after agents or tools fail — via exploit path, credential theft, spoofed summaries,
          or supply-chain slip — can your organization Patch, Revoke,
          and Reconstruct?
        
    
        
    
          This scorecard is for diligence on AI defense vendors and internal programs.
          It scores whether ops can close loops, not whether research benchmarks
          look impressive in isolation.
        
    

        
    Why research metrics ≠ ops outcomes
    
        
    
          Leaderboards, paper evals, and staged demos measure capability under curated
          conditions. They rarely prove that you can ship a fix from a known exploit path
          and keep it closed; kill agent credentials and confirm they stay dead; or rebuild
          what happened when logs are incomplete or adversarial.
        
    
        
    
          Independent evals that run on demoware — different models, tools, memory, or
          privilege model than production — inflate confidence. DEFOPS treats production
          fidelity and close-loop ownership as first-class dimensions.
        
    

        
    The seven dimensions
    
        
      
          
    1. Patch / close loop — Ship a fix from a known exploit path; prove it stays closed.
      2. 
          
    2. Agent identity & revocation — Kill agent/tool credentials; confirm they stay dead.
      3. 
          
    3. Trajectory & summary integrity — Logs/summaries resist spoof/omission; reconstruct events.
      4. 
          
    4. Credential & key lifecycle (AI APIs) — Rotate, scope, and detect leaks for model/tool keys.
      5. 
          
    5. Registry & publish controls — Who can publish tools/skills/agents; review gates; pins.
      6. 
          
    6. Shared-state / improvised-C2 detection — Catch collusion via shared memory, files, side channels.
      7. 
          
    7. Independent eval fidelity — Third-party/red-team evals match the production stack.
      8. 
        
    

        
    Verdict rules
    
        
      
          
    • Fund — mean ≥ 2.5 and no dimension at 0
      • 
          
    • Don’t fund — mean < 1.5 or ≥ 2 dimensions at 0
      • 
          
    • Gate — everything else (including incomplete assessments)
      • 
        
    
        
    
          Rubric: 0 Missing / unknown ·
          1 Manual / ad hoc ·
          2 Documented process, partial automation ·
          3 Measured, owned, tested regularly
